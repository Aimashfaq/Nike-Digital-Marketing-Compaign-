📢 Nike Digital Marketing Campaign

📌 Project Overview

This project presents a complete digital marketing campaign for Nike, a globally recognized sportswear brand. The campaign focuses on increasing brand engagement, improving customer interaction, and driving online sales through multiple digital marketing channels.

The campaign was designed as an academic practical project and demonstrates digital marketing strategy, creative design, content planning, and campaign execution.

🎯 Campaign Objective

Increase brand awareness and engagement
Drive online store traffic
Improve customer interaction
Boost online sales through digital channels
